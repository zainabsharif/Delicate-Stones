The Studio

By Daniya, bead by bead.

DelicateStones started as a small collection of beaded pieces made for friends. Every bracelet and necklace is still strung by hand, in small batches, using semi-precious stones, freshwater pearls, and gold-filled accents.

No two batches are identical — and that's the point. If you want something changed — length, color, a specific bead — just ask on WhatsApp before you order.

  • Made to order, usually within a few days
  • Adjustable sizing on most bracelets
  • Customization available — colors, charms, length
  • Packaged in a branded DelicateStones box
— Daniya
